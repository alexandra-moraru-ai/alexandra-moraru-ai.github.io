About

I learned risk management in pharmacovigilance, where adverse events had to be documented within 24 hours and a missed deadline was a compliance failure. A Master of Public Health added the population view, and an MSc in Cyber Security, now in progress, adds the technical one. I bring that mix to AI governance: I can follow the regulation, the security question and the patient safety concern in the same conversation, and I know how to turn a framework into a process people actually run. Healthcare AI governance is the specialism I am building on those foundations.
